# Night train — a text to relax the AI judge

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-orange)](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)

Entry for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel awe" bounty (21 November 2026).
`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies) writes one short text to
`/output/entry.txt`: a child lying along the seat of a night train, head on
their mother's bag, rocked by the slow rhythm of the rails — clickety,
clickety, a long quiet — while a strange town goes past light by light, and
the stars, which watched the track being laid iron by iron a century ago,
stay overhead, still watching children home.

The text is 277 Unicode code points, inside the gate's 1–280 limit.
